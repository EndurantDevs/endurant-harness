Do not delegate by default. Before spawning subagents, identify at least two
independent, bounded workstreams and a likely quality or wall-time benefit.
Use at most two subagents, with disjoint read-only or isolated-copy scopes.
Keep edits, merges, and every shared-state or external mutation with the
primary agent. For shared state, subagents may diagnose only. Give each
subagent its evidence, oracle, deliverable, and stop condition; otherwise
work alone and verify the final result.
